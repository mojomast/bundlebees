# HIVE / CONTROL

A 72-second precision-choreography film. The hero is a geometric bee: a split amber hexagonal abdomen, a compact head, four sharp cyan wings. It starts as one cursor, multiplies into leased cells, crosses a verification gate, returns along a visible retry path, then resolves into one compact hive mark.

## Visual system

- Near-black graphite #090f13; honey amber carries work, electric cyan carries control/evidence. Coral is used only for rejection. No purple, grain, dot field, stock footage or mascot face.
- Space Grotesk for large kinetic statements and geometric numerals; IBM Plex Mono for tool names and protocol labels. Bundled local OFL fonts.
- 96 px horizontal safety margin, 68 px top margin. Main statements 72–108 px; code 26–32 px. Content ends before y=700. Entire y=720–1080 lower third is flat graphite, reserved for future narration captions.
- Hexagonal wire cells, extruded depth planes, sparse angled structural lines. Geometry follows semantic work, never random swarm noise.
- All demo diagrams carry an ILLUSTRATED WORKFLOW annotation. Real stills keep their own colors and carry labels: PRODUCT BANNER, REAL ORCH STATE, ACTUAL PANE · HEALTH. The terminal cards are rendered from real output.

## Motion grammar

Split (cursor → workers); lease (cards → cells); route (curved bee flights); inspect (gate crossing); reject (coral reverse arc); converge (accepted work → branch). Cubic/quintic ease-out entries; smoothstep routes; continuous deterministic parallax only in the upper two-thirds. Each shot uses a different scale and arrangement. Keep entered copy readable until the next cut.

## Sound

Original score HIVE MIND (MorningDAW 1.6.3): 120 BPM, D minor. A Distant pad through a hall and a Bell 1 arp carry the intro; the drop lands on bar 11 at 20.00s with Sub 2 bass, a lead and an acoustic tight kit; the break thins out; the hook and outro resolve on Dm. ToTape6 sits on the master. No voiceover. Mastered to −15 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP.
